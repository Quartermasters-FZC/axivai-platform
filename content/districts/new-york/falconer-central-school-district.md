Falconer Central School District is one of the few New York districts whose bus garage is served by a municipal electric utility. Its main campus at 2 East Avenue, home to the Middle/High School, Fenner Elementary and the bus garage, sits in the territory of the Jamestown Board of Public Utilities (BPU). That changes who the district talks to about charger power. For the statewide rules and funding, see [New York electric school buses](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## District profile

- **Enrollment:** 1,036 students in 2024–25, down from 1,084 in 2023–24 and 1,089 in 2022–23 (NYSED). The district projects 980 for 2026–27.
- **Area:** 70.62 square miles in Chautauqua County, in NYSED's Western New York labor market region.
- **Schools:** the Middle/High School and Harvey C. Fenner Elementary share the 2 East Avenue campus in Falconer. Temple Elementary is in Kennedy.
- **Budget:** proposed 2026–27 spending is $35,372,000, up 8.74%, with the tax levy held flat at $7,133,890 (NYSED property tax report card).

## Fleet and transportation

The bus garage stands on the main campus at 2 East Avenue, next to two of the district's three schools. Any charger installation there shares a site, and possibly a service, with the school buildings.

The garage has been part of recent capital work:

- **Capital Improvement Project 2023, Phase 2.** Bids were due 14 February 2025. The project listed four locations: the Middle/High School, Fenner Elementary, Temple Elementary and the bus garage. The published scope covers HVAC, plumbing, fire alarm and toilet and locker room work. The plumbing contract alone was estimated at $771,000.
- **Capital Improvements Project, 2026.** This was Proposition 2 on the 19 May 2026 ballot, funded from a Capital Improvement Reserve Fund the district set up in May 2022.

Neither project's public description mentions chargers or charger conduit. NYSED's 2026–27 schedule of reserves lists no reserve for buses or vehicles.

## Depot power and the grid

**A municipal utility.** The state's electric utility territory data places the 2 East Avenue campus in Jamestown BPU territory. The BPU lists the Village of Falconer and the Town of Ellicott among the communities it serves. It generates power locally with gas and steam turbines and also buys hydropower from the New York Power Authority's Niagara project.

For charging, this matters in three ways:

- **Who to ask.** The district's service upgrade goes to the BPU, not to National Grid or NYSEG. NYSED notes that Western New York districts are mainly served by National Grid, NYSEG and RG&E, with a few small providers. Falconer is one of those few.
- **Which programs apply.** The district's fleet plan should confirm which charging incentives are open to a BPU customer.
- **Rates.** Overnight bus charging will be billed on BPU tariffs, so the BPU's rates set what charging costs to run.

The district's planning is moving. NYSED's October 2025 report lists Falconer's NYSERDA Fleet Electrification Plan as "Underway". Across Western New York, 18 of 80 districts had completed plans and 13 had made no contact. Chautauqua County neighbours are ahead on buses: Cassadaga Valley reported 4 zero-emission buses in a fleet of 27, and Pine Valley 2 of 23. Jamestown City SD has a completed plan.

For upstate grid timelines, see [New York's electric school-bus gap](/research/ny-implementation-gap).

## Funding

- **NYSBIP:** NYSERDA's project data, updated 26 October 2026, lists no vouchers for Falconer.
- **Capital reserves:** the 2026–27 schedule shows a $3.4 million capital reserve drawn down to zero and a further $1 million held for future capital projects.
- **Vehicles:** no dedicated bus reserve appears on the state schedule.

Program amounts and eligibility are on the [New York page](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## Where AXIVAI fits

If the Jamestown BPU needs time to upgrade service to the 2 East Avenue garage, AXIVAI can bring battery power by truck into storage on the yard so Falconer's first electric buses run in the meantime. AXIVAI is preparing its first pilot sites.
