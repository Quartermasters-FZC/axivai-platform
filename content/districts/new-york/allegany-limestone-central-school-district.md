Allegany-Limestone Central School District moved from zero to one electric bus in under a year. NYSED's October 2025 report listed the district as having made no contact with NYSERDA about a fleet plan. Two months later, NYSERDA approved a NYSBIP voucher for a battery-electric IC Bus, and the project closed in August 2026. For statewide rules and funding, see [New York electric school buses](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## District profile

- **Enrollment:** 987 students in 2024–25, down from 991 in 2023–24 and 1,020 in 2022–23 (NYSED). That is a 3% fall in two years.
- **Area:** 50.77 square miles in Cattaraugus County, in NYSED's Western New York labor market region. That is about 19 students per square mile, compact by rural standards.
- **Budget:** proposed 2026–27 spending is $29,854,715, up 2.68% from $29,074,607 (district budget; NYSED Property Tax Report Card).

## Fleet and transportation

Allegany-Limestone buys and runs its own buses. Its program budget shows the spending:

| Budget line | 2025–26 | 2026–27 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pupil transportation (operations) | $1,287,250 | $1,325,700 |
| Pupil transportation (bus purchase) | $672,306 | $325,000 |

Its first electric bus, per NYSERDA's NYSBIP data:

- **Make and model:** IC Bus CE Series, model year 2027.
- **Class:** Class 7, 26,001 to 33,000 lb, battery-electric.
- **Dealer:** Leonard Bus Sales.
- **Dates:** voucher approved 9 December 2025; project closed 21 August 2026.

The bus-purchase line falls by more than half for 2026–27. The budget does not break it down by vehicle.

## Depot power and the grid

One electric bus is the start. The harder question is power for the rest of the fleet. NYSED's October 2025 report lists the district's NYSERDA Fleet Electrification Plan status as "No Contact". In Western New York, 13 of 80 districts (16%) were in that position, while 18 (22%) had completed plans.

The fleet plan is free. It sizes chargers and the service upgrade for the whole fleet. Once its first bus has run a winter on Cattaraugus County routes, Allegany-Limestone can feed real range and charging data into that plan, rather than estimates.

The upstate grid picture is in [New York's electric school-bus gap](/research/ny-implementation-gap).

## Funding

- **NYSBIP:** one voucher of $196,000, approved 9 December 2025 and closed 21 August 2026. The district carries NYSBIP's priority-district flag; the bus is located outside a disadvantaged community (NYSERDA data, updated 26 October 2026).
- **Local:** bus purchases are budgeted in the general fund: $672,306 in 2025–26 and $325,000 in 2026–27.

Program amounts are on the [New York page](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## Where AXIVAI fits

Allegany-Limestone's first electric bus is funded; power for a growing electric fleet is the next constraint. When the next buses arrive before the utility upgrade, AXIVAI brings battery power by truck into storage at the garage so they can run in the meantime.
