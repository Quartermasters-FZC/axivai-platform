Boquet Valley does not yet know where its buses will live. The district was formed by merging Elizabethtown-Lewis and Westport, and it still runs two campuses in Essex County, in Elizabethtown and Westport. Its facilities committee has recommended that the Lake View Campus in Westport not be the permanent home of the transportation facility, and has asked landowners for a buildable site of at least 20 acres for a bus garage and athletic fields. If it builds, the new garage can be designed for electric buses from the start. For statewide rules and funding, see [New York electric school buses](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## District profile

- **Enrollment:** 366 students in 2024–25, against 365 in 2023–24 and 368 in 2022–23 (NYSED). Enrollment is stable.
- **Area:** 236.4 square miles, in NYSED's North Country labor market region.
- **Density:** about 1.5 students per square mile.
- **Campuses:** Mountain View Campus at 7530 Court Street, Elizabethtown, and Lake View Campus at 25 Sisco Street, Westport.
- **Budget:** voters approved the 2026–27 budget on 19 May 2026, 422 to 239.

## Fleet and transportation

Boquet Valley owns and runs its own buses and pays for them in cash from its Transportation and Maintenance Equipment Capital Reserve Fund, not by borrowing.

- **May 2025:** two 65-passenger school buses at up to $357,000, approved 410 to 184.
- **May 2026:** one 35-passenger bus and one 7-passenger van at up to $162,000, approved 485 to 175.

The Board of Education received a winter report on the bus fleet and its near-term priorities on 12 February 2026. NYSERDA's NYSBIP data, updated 26 October 2026, list no electric bus vouchers for the district.

## Depot power and the grid

The garage question has run through three years of facilities planning.

- **February 2024:** voters rejected a proposal to build a new school replacing both campuses on a different site.
- **November 2024:** a Building Condition Survey put the bare minimum to keep both campuses open at $20.5 million. The committee's notes said the bus garage "has some needs", and that much more work would be needed for an all-electric fleet. A garage not on a school site would get less state building aid, though some.
- **February 2025:** the committee looked for a site of 20 acres or more for fields and a bus garage. Reusing the Lake View Campus was ruled out on cost, because its buildings would have to be demolished first.
- **March 2025:** the committee recommended consolidating all classes at Mountain View Campus, keeping Lake View from being the permanent transportation site, and continuing to search for land for a new transportation facility. Transportation was left out of the near-term project because too much about it was uncertain.

A new garage is the cheapest point to plan for charging. Conduit, a right-sized electrical service and space for chargers cost less in a new building than in a retrofit. The district has not yet contacted NYSERDA about a free Fleet Electrification Plan, according to NYSED's October 2025 report. In the North Country, 23 of 65 districts (36%) were in the same position. A plan would size the charger load before the garage is designed.

For the upstate grid picture, see [New York's electric school-bus gap](/research/ny-implementation-gap) and [school-bus depot power](/research/school-bus-depots).

## Funding

- **NYSBIP:** no vouchers as of October 2026.
- **Reserves:** buses and vans are bought outright from the transportation and equipment reserve.
- **Building aid:** a garage on a non-school site would receive less state aid than one on a school campus.

Program rules are on the [New York page](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## Where AXIVAI fits

A new garage on a new site may wait years for a utility service sized for electric buses. AXIVAI can bring battery power by truck into storage at whichever yard the buses use, so the first electric buses can run before that connection is in place.
