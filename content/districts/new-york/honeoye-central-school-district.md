Honeoye's voters gave their clearest answer of 2026 not on the budget but on energy. On 19 May they approved an energy performance contract, 408 to 85, a far wider margin than the budget's 351 to 147. The district's NYSERDA fleet plan is under way, and Canandaigua, elsewhere in Ontario County, already has four vouchered electric buses. Honeoye CSD is a separate district from the larger Honeoye Falls-Lima CSD (2,044 students). For the statewide rules and funding, see [New York electric school buses](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## District profile

- **Enrollment:** 525 students in 2024–25, against 522 in 2023–24 and 529 in 2022–23 (NYSED). Enrollment is steady.
- **Need:** 45% of students are economically disadvantaged (NYSED, 2024–25).
- **Area:** 86.05 square miles, in NYSED's Finger Lakes labor market region.
- **Density:** about six students per square mile.
- **Budget:** the 2026–27 budget passed on 19 May 2026, 351 to 147 (NYSED).

## Fleet and transportation

Honeoye's buses cover more than 86 square miles. With about six students per square mile, routes are long for the number of riders, and winter range will be the first test for any electric bus.

The district's recent ballots have carried no bus propositions: none in May 2024, May 2025 or May 2026, according to NYSED's vote records. Any bus purchases in those years were made without a stand-alone proposition. If that continues, the first electric bus may arrive through the normal budget rather than a separate vote.

## Depot power and the grid

**The utility.** The school campus on East Main Street sits in National Grid's territory (New York State utility territory map).

**The energy performance contract.** An energy performance contract (EPC) pays for building energy upgrades from the savings they produce over time. Voters approved Honeoye's on 19 May 2026, 408 to 85 (NYSED). The vote record does not list the measures. An EPC is a natural moment to look at the campus electrical service as a whole: lighting, heating and controls that cut building load can free capacity for bus chargers on the same service. If the EPC's engineer and the fleet plan's consultant compare numbers, the district can avoid sizing the service twice.

**The plan.** NYSED's October 2025 report lists Honeoye's NYSERDA Fleet Electrification Plan (FEP) as "Underway". The Finger Lakes region is one of the furthest along: 16 of 70 districts (23%) had completed plans and 42 (60%) were under way, with only 10 (14%) making no contact.

**The neighbour's example.** Elsewhere in Ontario County, Canandaigua City School District holds four NYSBIP vouchers for Thomas Built Saf-T-Liner C2 Jouley battery-electric buses, worth $220,500 to $224,500 each. Two are in projects approved in February 2025 and now closed; two more were approved in March 2026 (NYSERDA).

## Funding

- **NYSBIP:** NYSERDA's project data, updated 26 October 2026, lists no vouchers for Honeoye.
- **Energy:** the energy performance contract approved in May 2026 is self-funding by design, paid from energy savings.

How NYSBIP vouchers combine with state aid is covered in the [NYSBIP report](/research/new-york-school-bus-incentive-program).

## Where AXIVAI fits

If Honeoye's fleet plan shows the campus needs more National Grid capacity than the energy work frees up, AXIVAI can bring battery power by truck into storage on the yard so the first electric buses run while the upgrade is built. AXIVAI is preparing its first pilot sites.
