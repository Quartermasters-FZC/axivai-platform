Holland Patent runs a big rural operation for a district of 1,160 students: 532,735 bus miles a year, 2,204 miles a day. In May 2026 voters approved four more buses and a $30.75 million capital project that includes an emergency backup generator at the bus garage. That generator is the first piece of resilience at the depot, and the right moment to ask whether the garage's electrical service can take chargers too. For the statewide rules and funding, see [New York electric school buses](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## District profile

- **Enrollment:** 1,160 students in 2024–25, against 1,176 in 2023–24 and 1,177 in 2022–23 (NYSED).
- **Need:** 38% of students are economically disadvantaged (NYSED, 2024–25).
- **Area:** 114.4 square miles, in NYSED's Mohawk Valley labor market region.
- **Density:** about ten students per square mile.
- **Schools:** General William Floyd Elementary, Holland Patent Elementary, Holland Patent Middle School and Holland Patent High School.
- **Budget:** voters approved a $45,330,604 budget for 2026–27 on 19 May 2026, 524 to 351, with a 3.25% levy increase under a 5.02% cap.

## Fleet and transportation

Holland Patent runs its own buses. Its January 2024 transportation presentation to the board gives the operation in numbers:

| Measure | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Students eligible for daily transportation | 1,292 |
| In-district routes | 17, run twice a day |
| Out-of-district placement routes | 6, run twice a day |
| Additional special routes | 9 |
| Miles a day | 2,204 (1,445 in district, 759 out of district) |
| Miles a year | 532,735 |
| Extracurricular miles, year to date | 17,519 |
| Staff | 23.5 drivers, 5 substitutes, 2.5 mechanics |

Two things stand out for electric buses. First, out-of-district runs make up a third of daily miles. Those are the longest trips and the hardest to cover on one winter charge. Second, 2,204 daily miles across 23 regular drivers is close to 100 miles per driver per day. Many runs will fit an electric bus's range, but each needs checking with the heater on in an Oneida County January.

Voters keep the fleet current. Bus propositions passed in May 2024 (507–173), May 2025 (578–257) and May 2026, when four buses for $573,841 passed 590 to 249 (district; NYSED).

## Depot power and the grid

The district's Main Street address is in National Grid's territory (New York State utility territory map).

**The 2026 capital project.** Voters approved the $30,750,000 project on 19 May 2026, 519 to 363. It is funded 93.6% by state building aid and 6.4% from the capital reserve, with no anticipated tax increase. Among its "additional scope items" is an emergency/backup generator for the bus garage. The project also adds generators at General William Floyd Elementary and the middle school, a middle school steam-to-hot-water conversion and district-wide HVAC control upgrades.

A backup generator keeps a garage working through an outage. It does not by itself add capacity for chargers. When the fleet plan sizes a charging load, the garage's main service and the new generator should be designed together, so the generator can also cover critical charging if the district wants it to.

**The plan.** NYSED's October 2025 report lists Holland Patent's NYSERDA Fleet Electrification Plan (FEP) as "Underway". In the Mohawk Valley, 33 of 54 districts (61%) were under way and 4 (7%) had completed plans. No bus in Oneida County appears in NYSERDA's NYSBIP voucher data as of October 2026.

## Funding

- **NYSBIP:** NYSERDA's project data, updated 26 October 2026, lists no vouchers for Holland Patent.
- **Buses:** four buses for $573,841 approved in May 2026, an average of about $143,500 each.
- **Capital:** the $30.75M project, 93.6% state building aid.

How NYSBIP vouchers combine with state aid is covered in the [NYSBIP report](/research/new-york-school-bus-incentive-program).

## Where AXIVAI fits

If the FEP finds the bus garage needs a larger National Grid service for chargers, AXIVAI can bring battery power by truck into storage at the garage so the first electric buses run while the upgrade is built, alongside the new backup generator. AXIVAI is preparing its first pilot sites.
