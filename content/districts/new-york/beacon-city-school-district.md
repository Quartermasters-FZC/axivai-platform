Beacon has already bought electric school buses. NYSERDA's data show two battery-electric buses vouchered to the district, worth $514,500 in all. The project was approved in April 2024 and closed in May 2026. Yet NYSED's October 2025 report lists Beacon as having made no contact with NYSERDA about a fleet plan. Beacon has started buying, but it hasn't yet planned the whole fleet. For statewide rules and funding, see [New York electric school buses](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## District profile

- **Enrollment:** 2,468 students in 2024–25, against 2,465 in 2023–24 and 2,470 in 2022–23 (NYSED). Enrollment is flat.
- **Area:** 26.14 square miles in Dutchess County, in NYSED's Hudson Valley labor market region. The district covers the City of Beacon and parts of the towns of Fishkill and Wappinger.
- **Density:** about 94 students per square mile.
- **Budget:** $91,850,000 for 2026–27, up 4.71% from $87,720,000. The tax levy rises 4.47% to $52,342,777 (NYSED property tax report card).

## Fleet and transportation

Beacon runs its own buses. Its 2026–27 budget book pays for a transportation director, dispatcher, head driver, drivers, mechanics and monitors. It also carries a separate line for the district's bus garage. The figures show the size of the operation:

| Item | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Destinations served | 6 district schools and more than 50 nonpublic programs |
| Students on district buses | 2,466 (2024–25), up from 2,100 (2023–24) |
| Regular-route mileage, district buses | 371,104 miles (2024–25); 375,000 projected (2025–26) |
| Fuel budget | $300,000 a year (2025–26 and 2026–27) |
| Total transportation budget | $3,994,968 (2026–27) |

Those 371,104 route miles are the useful number for planning. Spread across a fleet, they set the daily energy each electric bus must carry, and so the size of the chargers.

**Buying buses.** Beacon finances buses with bonds. The 19 May 2026 ballot asked voters to approve a bond resolution, adopted by the Board on 9 March 2026, for school buses at a maximum estimated cost of $640,000, repaid over five years.

## Depot power and the grid

The two vouchered buses are Navistar CE Series models, model year 2026, in weight class 7. NYSERDA lists the project as inside a disadvantaged community. NYSBIP lists only three other Dutchess districts with vouchers: Red Hook (6), Hyde Park (2) and Arlington (1).

Dutchess County is served by Central Hudson Gas and Electric and NYSEG (NYS Department of Public Service). A garage service that handles two chargers may not handle twenty.

That is where the missing fleet plan matters. NYSED's October 2025 report lists Beacon's FEP status as **no contact**. Across the Hudson Valley's 101 districts:

- 5 (5%) had a completed plan;
- 26 (26%) had one underway;
- 11 (11%) had made initial contact;
- 56 (56%), including Beacon, had made no contact.

An FEP would tell Beacon how far its bus garage's electrical service stretches beyond the first buses. See [New York's electric school-bus gap](/research/ny-implementation-gap).

## Funding

- **NYSBIP:** two buses at $257,250 each, $514,500 in all. Approved 10 April 2024 and closed 10 May 2026 (NYSERDA project data, updated 26 October 2026).
- **Local:** bus purchases are financed with five-year bonds; the 2026 proposition sought up to $640,000.

Program details are on the [New York page](/electric-school-buses/new-york) and in the [NYSBIP report](/research/new-york-school-bus-incentive-program).

## Where AXIVAI fits

When Beacon adds electric buses faster than its garage service can carry, AXIVAI can bring battery power by truck into storage on the yard so the new buses run while the utility upgrade is done. AXIVAI is preparing its first pilot sites.
