Brocton Central School District has done the planning many districts have not started. NYSED's October 2025 report lists its NYSERDA Fleet Electrification Plan as **complete**. Brocton also has an unusual power supplier. The state's utility territory map puts the village of Brocton, home to the district's schools, inside the Village of Brocton's own municipal electric system rather than National Grid territory. For statewide rules and funding, see [New York electric school buses](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## District profile

- **Enrollment:** 456 students in 2024–25, down from 473 in 2023–24 and 476 in 2022–23 (NYSED). That is a 4% fall in two years.
- **Need:** 64% of students are economically disadvantaged (NYSED, 2024–25).
- **Schools:** Brocton Elementary School and Brocton Middle High School (NYSED).
- **Area:** 58.26 square miles in northern Chautauqua County, near the Lake Erie shore, in NYSED's Western New York labor market region. That is about eight students per square mile.
- **Budget:** the 2026–27 state filing shows spending of $22,653,097, up 7.68%, with a tax levy up 1.72%.

## Fleet and transportation

Brocton owns and runs its own buses. NYSDOT recorded 41 safety inspections of its vehicles, one of them failed, in the state fiscal year ending 31 March 2026. Buses are inspected twice a year, which points to a fleet of about 20 buses and other vehicles.

The district saves ahead for buses. NYSED's 2026–27 schedule of reserves lists:

| Reserve | Balance (projected) | Stated purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bus reserve | about $778,000 | "To offset the local share of future bus purchases" |
| Capital reserve | about $802,000 | "To offset the local share of a future capital project" |

The same filing links its unemployment reserve to "the potential decrease in staff related to declining enrollment". Enrollment pressure shapes the fleet as well. Fewer riders can mean fewer or smaller buses by the 2030s, when new purchases must be zero-emission.

## Depot power and the grid

Brocton's power supply is the most distinctive thing about its electric-bus outlook. On the state's utility territory map, the village is served by **Municipal Utility: Brocton**, a small village-owned system, while the land around the village falls in National Grid territory.

That has practical effects:

- **Who to call.** Load letters, service upgrades and any new transformer for bus charging go through the village utility, not a large investor-owned utility.
- **Scale.** A bank of bus chargers would be a large new load for a small village system, so lead times for transformers and switchgear matter.

Brocton has the plan needed to start those conversations. Its NYSERDA Fleet Electrification Plan is listed as complete. In Western New York only 18 of 80 districts (22%) had reached that stage, and 13 (16%) had made no contact with NYSERDA. A finished plan sets out charger counts, route energy and the power the depot needs. Those are the numbers the village utility will ask for.

Winter is the other constraint. Brocton sits in the Lake Erie snow belt, where cold mornings cut battery range and preheating adds load. Large power transformers can take two to four years to arrive (NIAC, June 2024). For the wider upstate grid picture, see [New York's electric school-bus gap](/research/ny-implementation-gap).

## Funding

- **Bus reserve:** about $778,000 set aside for the local share of future buses.
- **Capital reserve:** about $802,000 for a future capital project, which could cover depot electrical work.
- **NYSBIP:** NYSERDA's project data, updated 26 October 2026, lists no vouchers for Brocton. In Chautauqua County, Cassadaga Valley, Pine Valley, Bemus Point, Jamestown and Silver Creek have NYSBIP vouchers.

Amounts and eligibility for the state programs are on the [New York page](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## Where AXIVAI fits

If the village utility needs time to add capacity for Brocton's chargers, AXIVAI can bring battery power by truck into storage at the bus garage so the first electric buses run before the upgrade is finished. AXIVAI is preparing its first pilot sites.
