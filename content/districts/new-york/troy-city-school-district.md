Troy does not own its buses, but it has already put money aside for the switch. In September 2025 the Board of Education's agenda carried a resolution to move up to $2 million of fund balance into a reserve "for the purpose of Electric Bus Transition Funding". Seven months later the district changed bus contractors. For statewide rules and funding, see [New York electric school buses](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## District profile

- **Enrollment:** 3,475 students in 2024–25, against 3,472 in 2023–24 and 3,491 in 2022–23 (NYSED). Enrollment is flat.
- **Area:** 10.45 square miles, in NYSED's Capital Region labor market region.
- **Density:** about 330 students per square mile. Routes are short and urban, and part of the school-day ridership already uses public transit.
- **Budget:** voters approved a 2026–27 budget of $146,348,609 on 19 May 2026, 639 to 72. It holds the tax levy flat.
- **A merger is coming.** Wynantskill Union Free School District voters approved annexation to Troy on 31 March 2026. The expected start date is 1 July 2027. The district says the reorganization will bring about $241 million in state incentive aid over 15 years.

## Fleet and transportation

Troy buys transportation as a service. Its 2026–27 budget statement shows the split:

| Budget line | 2025–26 adopted | 2026–27 proposed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract transportation | $8,492,000 | $9,517,000 |
| Public transportation (CDTA) | $400,000 | $400,000 |
| District transportation services | $302,619 | $310,234 |
| **Total pupil transportation** | **$9,194,990** | **$10,227,616** |

Contract costs rise 12% in one year.

**The contractor changed in 2026.** Durham School Services ran Troy's buses through 2025–26, on a one-year extension put to the board on 9 July 2025 at a 3.4% CPI increase. The district then issued a request for proposals. Bids were opened on 18 March 2026. The board's 21 April 2026 agenda awarded home-to-school, athletic, field-trip and summer school routes to Student Transportation of America (STA), at $6,350,940 for 2026–27. The adopted budget describes it as a new five-year contract, and STA now handles day-to-day bus questions for families.

**High school students ride city buses.** Unless they are assigned to an STA route, Troy High students travel on the Capital District Transportation Authority (CDTA). From fall 2026 they get a free pass for the 802 Troy Shuttle; other CDTA routes now require a full fare.

## Depot power and the grid

Troy sits in National Grid's upstate service territory (NYS electric utility territory map). The buses that carry Troy students park at contractor yards, not on district land. So charging for Troy's future electric buses will depend on the yard STA uses and on the service National Grid can deliver there.

Two numbers frame the task:

- **A five-year contract signed in 2026** runs to about 2031. The next contract will be bid close to the 1 July 2032 purchase deadline. What it requires about electric buses and yard power matters more than any single vote.
- **No fleet plan yet.** NYSED's October 2025 progress report lists Troy as having made **no contact** with NYSERDA about a Fleet Electrification Plan. Across the Capital Region, 31 of 74 districts (42%) were in the same position. Only two had a completed plan.

For a contracted district, a fleet plan should model route energy for the STA routes and set out what the contractor must provide on yard power. The wider picture is in [New York's electric school-bus gap](/research/ny-implementation-gap).

## Funding

- **Electric bus reserve:** up to $2,000,000 of fund balance to the liability reserve, marked for electric bus transition funding (board agenda, 24 September 2025).
- **Capital reserve:** voters created a new capital reserve fund of up to $6 million on 19 May 2026, 625 to 84. After the Wynantskill annexation, Troy expects up to 98% state building aid on capital projects voted within ten years of the merger date.
- **NYSBIP:** NYSERDA's project data, updated 26 October 2026, lists no vouchers for Troy.

Program details are on the [New York page](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## Where AXIVAI fits

When Troy's contractor yard needs more power than its National Grid service allows, AXIVAI can bring battery power by truck into storage on the yard so new electric buses can run while the upgrade is built. AXIVAI is preparing its first pilot sites.
