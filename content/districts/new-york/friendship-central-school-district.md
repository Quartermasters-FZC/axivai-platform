Friendship's bus garage is in the middle of a renovation. The district's 2024 Capital Improvement Project covers the main school and the bus garage at 14 East Water Street, and its bid packages include electrical work. That makes now the cheapest moment to prepare the garage for chargers. Money is tight: the 2026–27 budget passed by a single vote. For the statewide rules and funding, see [New York electric school buses](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## District profile

- **Enrollment:** 241 students in 2024–25, down from 248 in 2023–24 and 261 in 2022–23 (NYSED). That is an 8% fall in two years.
- **Need:** 70% of students are economically disadvantaged (NYSED, 2024–25).
- **Area:** 41.66 square miles, in NYSED's Western New York labor market region.
- **Density:** about six students per square mile.
- **Budget:** the 2026–27 budget passed on 19 May 2026 by 48 votes to 47 (NYSED).

## Fleet and transportation

Friendship owns its buses and asks voters to approve replacements most years. NYSED's budget vote records show:

| Vote | Proposition | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2024 | Bus purchase and use of the transportation reserve fund | Passed 168–55 |
| May 2025 | School bus | Passed 63–14 |
| May 2025 | School facilities | Passed 62–15 |
| May 2026 | School bus purchase | Passed 44–19 |
| May 2026 | School facilities (two propositions) | Passed 53–9 and 47–14 |

The bus propositions draw much wider margins than the budget itself. Voters back replacing buses, which is how electric buses will eventually arrive here.

## Depot power and the grid

**The garage project.** On 4 November 2025 the district invited bids for general construction, roofing, plumbing, mechanical and electrical work on the 2024 Capital Improvements Project. The work covers the main building at 46 West Main Street and the bus garage at 14 East Water Street. Bids were due on 4 December 2025 (Olean Times Herald). The public notices do not describe the scope of the electrical work at the garage. Any charger conduit or panel capacity added during this project would cost much less than a retrofit later.

**The utility.** Both the school and the garage sit in Rochester Gas and Electric's territory, not National Grid's or NYSEG's (New York State utility territory map). Its review of the garage service will set the cost of the first chargers.

**The plan.** NYSED's October 2025 report lists Friendship's NYSERDA Fleet Electrification Plan (FEP) as "Underway". In Western New York, 43 of 80 districts (54%) were under way and 18 (22%) had completed plans. The same report says the region's districts generally find their electric service sufficient for 2025–26, but 33 expect to need upgrades for a fully electric fleet.

No bus in Allegany County appears in NYSERDA's NYSBIP voucher data as of October 2026.

## Funding

- **NYSBIP:** NYSERDA's project data, updated 26 October 2026, lists no vouchers for Friendship.
- **Local:** bus purchases are approved separately each spring, partly from a transportation reserve fund.
- **Capital:** the 2024 Capital Improvements Project covers the garage renovation.

For a district this size, the gap between a diesel bus and an electric bus after NYSBIP and state aid is the deciding number. The [NYSBIP report](/research/new-york-school-bus-incentive-program) explains how the voucher and aid combine.

## Where AXIVAI fits

If RG&E needs time to upgrade the East Water Street garage's service, AXIVAI can bring battery power by truck into storage at the garage so Friendship's first electric bus can run in the meantime. AXIVAI is preparing its first pilot sites.
