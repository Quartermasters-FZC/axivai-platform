Oswego's school buses have a second job. The district lies within 10 miles of a nuclear plant, and the evacuation plan put in place by FEMA relies on its buses to carry residents to the New York State Fairgrounds in an evacuation. In May 2026 the superintendent told the Oswego Rotary Club that electric buses could not do that in time, and that the district was working with its state senator to seek an extension of the mandate. For statewide rules and funding, see [New York electric school buses](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## District profile

- **Enrollment:** 3,459 students in 2024–25, against 3,513 in 2023–24 and 3,478 in 2022–23 (NYSED).
- **Area:** 57.51 square miles, in NYSED's Central New York labor market region.
- **Density:** about 60 students per square mile.
- **Budget:** the 2025–26 budget was $116,640,154, a $4.49 million increase with no rise in property taxes. The 2026–27 budget, which passed on 19 May 2026, cuts $1.3 million and 20 positions, 18 of them through attrition.
- **Nuclear host county:** Oswego County hosts three nuclear reactors, according to the county legislature.

## Fleet and transportation

Oswego owns and runs its fleet from its Transportation Department at 22 Richard Benjamin Blvd.

- **Replacement policy:** district policy is to replace nine buses each year. The superintendent says the cost is offset by state aid and lower maintenance.
- **Votes:** voters approved vehicle purchase propositions in May 2025 and May 2026, alongside the budget (WSYR results).
- **Evacuation role:** under the evacuation plan put in place by FEMA, Oswego's school buses would move people to the New York State Fairgrounds.

A steady nine-a-year cycle means the fleet turns over in roughly a decade. Buses bought in 2026 should be retired by the time the 2040 full-fleet date arrives. The hard part is the transition years after 2032, when every new bus must be zero-emission.

**Why the evacuation role matters.** An evacuation run is a long one-way trip, possibly in winter, with a return trip and no guarantee of charging at the far end. The amended law allows up to two 24-month extensions. A fleet plan could also test a middle path: keep a reserve of fuelled buses for emergency duty while electric buses take the daily routes.

## Depot power and the grid

Oswego sits in National Grid's territory (NYS electric utility territory map). Oswego County hosts three nuclear reactors, but nearby generation does not give a bus garage more capacity. That depends on the distribution feeder serving 22 Richard Benjamin Blvd.

Oswego has not begun the state's planning process. NYSED's October 2025 progress report lists the district as having made **no contact** with NYSERDA about a Fleet Electrification Plan (FEP). Across Central New York, 20 of 49 districts (41%) were in the same position, and only three (6%) had finished a plan.

An FEP for Oswego would need to cover two things other districts don't face:

- **Emergency duty.** How many buses must stay ready for an evacuation, and with what fuel or charge.
- **Garage capacity.** A nine-bus-a-year cycle, once it turns electric, adds chargers quickly. A load letter from National Grid shows when the garage service runs out.

For why upstate grid upgrades take years, see [New York's electric school-bus gap](/research/ny-implementation-gap) and the [FAQ](/faq).

## Funding

- **Capital planning:** the district's long-range facility plan aims to level capital spending at about $25 million a year. Capital projects are funded from aid and reserves with no tax impact.
- **Bus aid:** the superintendent says the cost of the nine-bus cycle is offset by state aid and lower maintenance costs.
- **NYSBIP:** NYSERDA's project data, updated 26 October 2026, lists no vouchers for Oswego.

Program details are on the [New York page](/electric-school-buses/new-york).

## Where AXIVAI fits

AXIVAI brings battery power to a bus garage by truck, so Oswego's first electric buses could run while National Grid upgrades the garage service, and that stored power can also back up the yard in an emergency. AXIVAI is preparing its first pilot sites.
